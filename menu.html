Good to Know

❖
  • Please inform our staff of any food allergies or dietary restrictions before ordering — we're happy to accommodate where we can.
  • Spice levels can be adjusted mild, medium, or hot upon request.
  • Consuming raw or undercooked meat, poultry, seafood, shellfish, or eggs may increase your risk of foodborne illness.
  • A 20% gratuity is automatically added to parties of 6 or more.
  • Our restaurant is GMO free, and we only use halal meat.
  • Prices are subject to change without notice and do not include applicable sales tax.
  • We reserve the right to refuse service to anyone.
