Our Story

A table built on the Himalaya

High in Nepal and across the Himalaya, a shared meal is never rushed. At Yak & Spice, we've carried that spirit down to Piedmont Avenue — hand-pleated momo, dal simmered low and slow, bread pulled fresh from the clay tandoor.

Every dish on our table draws from Nepal, Kashmir, and the wider Himalayan range: recipes passed down through kitchens, not looked up — served in a dining room built for lingering over one more plate.
